Sections 03 through 07 of the Ghana Gas Site Brief capture monthly operational updates from each department. Every department update card includes a department name, a status badge, a free-text update body, and metadata fields such as lead officer, key dates, and reference numbers. All fields are editable directly in the browser.

## Card anatomy

Each department update card is made up of three areas:

**Header row**

The top of the card contains the department icon, the department name, and the status badge. Click the department name to rename it. Click the status badge to change the label text.

**Update body**

The main content area holds the narrative update for the reporting period. Click anywhere in the body text to edit it. This is where you describe activities completed, work in progress, issues raised, and actions planned.

**Metadata row**

At the bottom of the card, a row of two or three labeled fields provides structured data for the entry — for example, Lead Officer, Next Forum, Contractor, Report Ref, or Drill Date. Click the value beneath any label to edit it. Labels are fixed per card but values are fully editable.

### Status badge colors

The badge in the top-right of each card uses color to signal the current operational state:

| Color | Example labels | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Green | Active, Normal, Satisfactory | Activities are proceeding as expected. No issues. |
| Amber | Ongoing, In Progress, Monitoring, Action Required, Attention, Active Works | Work is underway or a matter is being tracked. |
| Blue | Scheduled, Procurement | An activity is planned but not yet started. |
| Red | Alert | An issue requires immediate escalation or intervention. |

## Tips for writing updates

<Tip>
Write updates in the past tense for completed activities and present or future tense for ongoing or scheduled items. Replace all `[placeholder]` text before exporting or printing the brief.
</Tip>

Keep each update focused on the facts for the reporting period: what was done, what is in progress, what is planned next, and any issues or risks to flag. Avoid carrying over unchanged text from the previous month without reviewing it — the status badges and metadata dates are easy to overlook.

<Note>
The number of cards per department section is fixed in the current version. To add or remove cards, edit the HTML source.
</Note>
