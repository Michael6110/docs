The Ghana Gas Site Brief is an interactive, browser-based reporting tool used by Ghana National Gas Company Limited's Technical Department to compile and distribute monthly operational site reports. Open the brief in any modern browser, fill in your data by clicking any field, and export a polished PDF in minutes — no software installation required.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Site Brief">
    Open `index.html` in your browser. The brief loads immediately with sample data pre-filled.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit your data">
    Click any text field, project name, status badge, or flow rate value to edit it directly in the browser.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your progress">
    Click **Save Progress** in the action bar at the bottom of the page. Your data is stored in the browser's local storage and reloaded automatically next time you open the file.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export to PDF">
    Click **Print / Export PDF** in the action bar, then choose "Save as PDF" in your browser's print dialog.
  </Step>
</Steps>
